# Twemoji Country Flags

`TwemojiCountryFlags.woff2` draws the country flags in the timezone and
language pickers. Windows has no flag emoji of its own: without this font a
flag shows as two letters ("FR").

- The flag artwork is from [Twemoji](https://github.com/twitter/twemoji),
  Copyright 2019 Twitter, Inc and other contributors, licensed under
  [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). It is unchanged,
  apart from being cut down to the flags.
- The cut-down font is from TalkJS's
  [country-flag-emoji-polyfill](https://github.com/talkjs/country-flag-emoji-polyfill)
  0.1.10, Copyright (c) 2022 TalkJS, under the MIT licence.
